Privacy Policy

Prime Kent Dev Hub (Nexora Nemesis Pairing). Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google or email we receive your email address, name and profile picture. When you link a WhatsApp number we store that number and its linked-device session so your bot can run.

How we use it

Only to log you in, show your account page and run your bot. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase. Bot sessions are stored on our bot server.

Deleting your data

You can unlink numbers and delete your account at any time from the Account page. Deleting your account removes your profile and linked numbers.

Contact
